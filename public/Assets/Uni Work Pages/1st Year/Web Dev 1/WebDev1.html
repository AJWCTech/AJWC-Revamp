Intro to Computing

Research and practical web development focusing on ethical computing, online communication, and web standards. The project demonstrates semantic HTML, CSS styling, accessibility, and media integration as required by the brief.

Executive Summary

This research examines ethical and responsible computing in the digital age, analysing challenges such as privacy, cyber misuse, and online communication. The project applies web development techniques including semantic markup, responsive styling, and accessible design to meet both academic and professional standards. The outcome demonstrates practical implementation of web technologies while addressing critical computing concepts.
